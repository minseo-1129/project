## screen.ob1.title <!-- screen.ob1.title -->

Goal · challenges

## screen.ob1.caption <!-- screen.ob1.caption -->

Starts with users’ challenges. Their choices shape goal defaults and priorities in weekly reports.

## screen.ob1.body <!-- screen.ob1.body -->

The first screen is a question, not a feature tour. The difficulties chosen here set the defaults of the goal screens and the order of emphasis in the report.

## screen.ob2.title <!-- screen.ob2.title -->

Goal · focus areas

## screen.ob2.caption <!-- screen.ob2.caption -->

Introduces six colour-coded activity areas in onboarding and uses them consistently throughout the app. Grey marks unlogged time.

## screen.ob2.body <!-- screen.ob2.body -->

Showing the six-colour palette in onboarding teaches from the start that each colour means the same thing across the app. Grey is unlogged time, so it is not selectable.

## screen.ob3.title <!-- screen.ob3.title -->

Wake & sleep times

## screen.ob3.caption <!-- screen.ob3.caption -->

Uses wake and bedtimes to anchor the 24-hour grid and estimated sleep, displaying planned sleep duration.

## screen.ob3.body <!-- screen.ob3.body -->

Wake and sleep times start the 24-cell grid and anchor the automatic sleep estimate. Planned sleep is calculated and shown immediately.

## screen.ob4.title <!-- screen.ob4.title -->

Goal · time allocation

## screen.ob4.caption <!-- screen.ob4.caption -->

Shows target hours as proportions of a 24-hour stacked bar and warns users when the total exceeds a day.

## screen.ob4.body <!-- screen.ob4.body -->

The original showed how many time blocks to fill next to the time input. That is gone; a stacked bar shows the share of 24 hours instead and warns on the spot when the total goes over.

## screen.rec.title <!-- screen.rec.title -->

Daily log

## screen.rec.caption <!-- screen.rec.caption -->

Gives sensor-based logging checks a compact, focused card.

## screen.rec.body <!-- screen.rec.body -->

In the original, an empty circle (the agent image) and O / X buttons took half the screen — an unbuilt feature owned the layout. Only the passive-data check really remained, so it stays as a single card.

## screen.d1.title <!-- screen.d1.title -->

Diary · mood & ratin

## screen.d1.caption <!-- screen.d1.caption -->

Divides diary questions into three short steps, each designed to fit on one screen.

## screen.d1.body <!-- screen.d1.body -->

The original stacked seven items — satisfaction, mood, meals, weather, leisure, study and sleep quality — on one long scrolling screen. Split into three steps, each fits on one screen.

## screen.d2.title <!-- screen.d2.title -->

Diary · meals & weather

## screen.d2.caption <!-- screen.d2.caption -->

Groups meals and weather into three-option selections to make factual entries quick and reduce taps.

## screen.d2.body <!-- screen.d2.body -->

Meals and weather are facts that need no judgement, so they are grouped as three-way choices to keep taps to a minimum.

## screen.d3.title <!-- screen.d3.title -->

Diary · daily ratings

## screen.d3.caption <!-- screen.d3.caption -->

Uses a consistent five-point scale for leisure satisfaction, academic achievement and sleep quality.

## screen.d3.body <!-- screen.d3.body -->

Leisure satisfaction, sense of achievement in study and sleep quality share one five-point scale. Mixing different scales means re-reading them every day.

## screen.stat.title <!-- screen.stat.title -->

Stats · daily life

## screen.stat.caption <!-- screen.stat.caption -->

Places a life-balance summary above the chart and labels goal-achievement counts directly on the trend.

## screen.stat.body <!-- screen.stat.body -->

The life-balance sentence now sits above the graph. Dots in the goal-achievement trend below carry their counts, so nobody has to count them.

## screen.statm.title <!-- screen.statm.title -->

Stats · mood

## screen.statm.caption <!-- screen.statm.caption -->

Combines three mood measures in a seven-day heatmap, with week-on-week changes and an option to compare daily activity data.

## screen.statm.body <!-- screen.statm.body -->

The three measures are merged into one seven-day heatmap. It shows which day what rose more clearly than three sets of bars, and the change against last week next to the weekly average answers “is it changing?”. Below, a switch overlays life data on the same date axis.

## screen.hl_sleep.title <!-- screen.hl_sleep.title -->

Sleep highlight

## screen.hl_sleep.caption <!-- screen.hl_sleep.caption -->

Prioritises daily sleep totals to support habit formation, using recommended sleep duration as a reference.

## screen.hl_sleep.body <!-- screen.hl_sleep.body -->

Sleep is about building a habit, so the daily total matters. The default summary window is daily, judged against the objective recommended hours of sleep.

## screen.hl_ex.title <!-- screen.hl_ex.title -->

Exercise highlight

## screen.hl_ex.caption <!-- screen.hl_ex.caption -->

Shows a weekly exercise summary and measures progress against each user’s session goal rather than general guidelines.

## screen.hl_ex.body <!-- screen.hl_ex.body -->

The weekly summary comes first, and the reference moves from the recommended amount to the user's own goal. The baseline is the goal per session, and the status label says “goal met”, not “too much”.

## screen.hl_leisure.title <!-- screen.hl_leisure.title -->

## screen.hl_leisure.body <!-- screen.hl_leisure.body -->

Only leisure keeps the recommended reference. The bar is the daily total of media leisure; its make-up is read separately in the “leisure mix” list below.

## screen.hl_study.title <!-- screen.hl_study.title -->

Uses a daily study summary and evaluates progress against the user’s own goal.

## screen.hl_study.body <!-- screen.hl_study.body -->

## screen.report.title <!-- screen.report.title -->

Weekly report

## screen.report.caption <!-- screen.report.caption -->

Uses restrained typography and generous spacing to present a seven-day, 24-hour activity grid on one screen

## screen.report.body <!-- screen.report.body -->

A centred title with a short black underline and generous space — the tone of the original report draft, brought into the app. The 7-day × 24-hour grid fits on one screen.

## screen.my.title <!-- screen.my.title -->

My · data controls

## screen.my.caption <!-- screen.my.caption -->

Makes My Page a hub for reviewing automatically collected data, connection status and controls.

## screen.my.body <!-- screen.my.body -->

The original had a fourth tab with no screen behind it. On a platform where half the data is collected passively, what people need first is “what is being collected” and “can I turn it off”, so My page is defined as data management.

## screen.my_data.title <!-- screen.my_data.title -->

Data connection

## screen.my_data.caption <!-- screen.my_data.caption -->

Lists Health Connect and device sensors with their data uses. Disabling a source switches that area to manual logging.

## screen.my_data.body <!-- screen.my_data.body -->

Health Connect and device sensors sit in one list, each noting which value it becomes and which life area it feeds. Toggles apply immediately; turning one off moves that area to manual logging.

## screen.my_goal.title <!-- screen.my_goal.title -->

Edit goals

## screen.my_goal.caption <!-- screen.my_goal.caption -->

Reuses familiar onboarding controls so users can adjust their goals at any time.

## screen.my_goal.body <!-- screen.my_goal.body -->

A goal is not set once and forgotten. The same controls as onboarding step 4 are reused, so the learned interaction carries over.

## screen.my_report.title <!-- screen.my_report.title -->

Report delivery time

## screen.my_report.caption <!-- screen.my_report.caption -->

Lets users choose the day and time for weekly report notifications to suit their routine.

## screen.my_report.body <!-- screen.my_report.body -->

Users choose the day and time. The report is the only content that arrives by push, so when it arrives decides whether it is opened.

## screen.my_archive.title <!-- screen.my_archive.title -->

Report archive

## screen.my_archive.caption <!-- screen.my_archive.caption -->

Archives weekly reports for long-term reflection, previewing each week’s logging rate and key takeaway.

## screen.my_archive.body <!-- screen.my_archive.body -->

Weekly reports pile up as a list so long-term change is visible, not just week-on-week comparison. Each entry previews that week's logging rate and one key sentence.

## screen.my_privacy.title <!-- screen.my_privacy.title -->

Privacy policy

## screen.my_privacy.caption <!-- screen.my_privacy.caption -->

## screen.my_privacy.body <!-- screen.my_privacy.body -->

Data collected, purpose, retention and third-party sharing are laid out in a table, with consent for research use as its own toggle. Because the service is for minors, guardian consent status is shown too.
